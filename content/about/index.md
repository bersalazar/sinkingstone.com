I'm Bernardo.

In this site I write about whatever excites me.
You'll find mostly ruminations of stuff I've read in books, seen in films or simply experienced.

Spanish is my native tongue but I spend a large percentage of my days thinking in english.
That's why I write in either.

I'm a [software engineer](https://salazr.dev), a [film enthusiast](https://letterboxd.com/sinkingstone) and spare-time [music maker](https://linktr.ee/berniroco).
I also enjoy [books](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/8994743-bernardo).

I never use writing assistance tools and I've stopped using spelling correction long ago.
Everything written here is from my own fingers, typing on a keyboard.
I embrace typos and grammar mistakes, re-visit and edit with fury.

I truly enjoy deep-dive and insightful conversations, learning other's thoughts (specially on topics I have major interest in) and witnessing people processing thoughts and opinions openly, trying to make sense of things.
If you find anything of what's written here exciting or curious, and want to talk about it, [email me](mailto:hola@sinkingstone.com).
